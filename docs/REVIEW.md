# Review and safeguarding

Keep literal retrieval, observations, and professional assessment separate. A message can mention a concerning behavior without demonstrating it. Verify who is speaking, whether they describe themselves or others, and whether the message reports abuse, quotes research, rejects violence, or expresses intent. Do not attribute quoted threats to the person reporting them.

For a case that warrants review, record only necessary information: original evidence and provenance, observation time, speaker and target roles, specific reason for concern, corroboration, change over time, alternative explanations, protective circumstances, reviewer rationale, and follow-up. Restrict access and retention. Do not place real victim data, private messages, explicit abuse imagery, or credentials into this public-source dataset. Do not infer ideology from protected traits or diagnosis.

## Past-shooter research and preparation

The [FBI prevention report](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/reports-and-publications/making-prevention-a-reality.pdf/download) discusses research, planning, and preparation. Review a documented connection to intended harm, rather than assuming that looking up a past attacker proves preparation. Reporting, prevention training, schoolwork, and historical interest are common explanations. Observation prompts are questions for humans; they are not automatically activated by term matches.

## Explosives-related inquiry and procurement

[ATF guidance](https://www.atf.gov/explosives/tools-services-explosives-industry/current-licensees/suspicious-or-dangerous-items) recognizes legitimate explosives use. [Home Office guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/supplying-explosives-precursors/supplying-explosives-precursors-and-poison) discusses suspicious transactions and missing inventory. Review patterns and corroborated context, not a molecule or product name alone. General retail anomalies are not specific to NVE or automatically criminal.

Relevant observations may concern an unexplained transaction purpose, inconsistent order patterns, identification concerns, unusual delivery requests, significant loss/theft, or access to restricted storage. Nervousness, cash payments, privacy preferences, or a single lawful purchase alone are weak evidence. No recipe, material combination, concentration threshold, brand directory, or source of supply is needed for this public moderation collection.

## Response

Use the forum's safeguarding and threat-response process for credible threats, coercion, and exploitation. Distinguish potential victims from people directing abuse. A credible immediate danger requires prompt attention through the appropriate local emergency process; do not wait for a keyword match, a checklist total, or professional assessment. Do not confront a suspected offender or handle a suspected explosive item. No reports or messages are sent automatically by this project.

## Retrieval limits

The reference matcher applies NFKC, case folding, removal of Unicode format characters, and whitespace collapse. It uses Unicode letter/digit boundaries; underscores act as separators. It does not recognize all inflections, translate text, resolve negation, infer intent, inspect images, or track behavior over time. Maintain original text separately because normalization changes offsets. TXT exports have no contextual intelligence.
