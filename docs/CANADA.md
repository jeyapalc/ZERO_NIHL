# Canadian scope and registry

The project masterlist is organized by subject, not translation targets. Foreign-language names belong where cited evidence establishes a Canadian connection or a clearly labelled background reference. Language, nationality, religion, and identity do not establish extremism.

The shared registry was reconciled against both official English and French web registers on 2026-11-17: 89 names in each language. These are localized records, not 178 distinct entities. The XML feeds still advertise 2025-12-10; the current snapshot uses the web pages. Names matched the previous snapshot; two French raw-alias strings were corrected. Original descriptions are not republished. See `data/canada/metadata.json` for source URLs and SHA-256 hashes.

Legal listing is not proof of operation in Canada or IMVE classification. The registry spans categories and remains separate from the project masterlist. Local IDs are language-specific and must not be used to join translations. Alias paragraphs remain unsplit unless individually verified in the project vocabulary.

Verified vocabulary updates: Atomwaffen Division's National Socialist Order/NSO aliases appear in Public Safety Canada's register; that entry expressly describes expansion into Canada. Maniac Murder Cult's transliterated aliases are in the same register; CSIS 2025 reporting supplies a Canadian connection. ZERO_FAITH adds Hide & Stalk from CSIS's 2025 report. Its French Quebec references are disabled historical research entries from the 2019 academic-outreach report, not current terrorist designations.

General German project translations and international background terms remain explicitly labelled in the masterlist. They are not claimed as observed Canadian extremist usage. More languages may be included when exact wording and Canadian relevance are supported by sources. See the per-row evidence and source records.
