# Canadian coverage

The separate `data/canada/lte_entities.json` registry captures canonical English and French names, unsplit official aliases, listing dates, raw review dates, and exact official URLs. It contains 89 English and 89 French records, not their sum in distinct entities. English feed IDs were reconciled against the official web register. The feeds report an update date of 2025-12-10; retrieval is dated 2026-11-03. Check the live source for changes. No entity descriptions or operational material are republished.

English and French source IDs must **never** be used to join translations: the same ID can identify different entities in the two feeds. This release retains separate language-scoped IDs. Alias text is preserved without attempting unreliable punctuation splitting. Names are contextual review references, not proof of support, association, criminality, or identity. Some are ordinary phrases or personal names. No automatic ban follows from a listing-name match.

All Canadian LTEs are included here regardless of ideological category. Their presence does not classify them as neo-Nazi, NVE, IMVE, or religiously motivated. Research labels and Canadian legal designation are separate fields and datasets. Canada's IMVE, PMVE, and RMVE policy categories are distinct and can overlap in individual cases. German classification labels are not mechanically equivalent.

Credible immediate danger in Canada requires prompt emergency attention through 9-1-1. The project sends no external reports.
