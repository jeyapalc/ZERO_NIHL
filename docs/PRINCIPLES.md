# General behavioral review principles

Review direct observations, context, changes over time, corroboration, and plausible alternatives. Distinguish a threat made by a speaker from a threat quoted or reported by a victim. Link research or preparation to evidence of intended harm before drawing conclusions. Record protective circumstances and de-escalation alongside concerns.

These principles are not a clinical test or validated prediction tool. A word match cannot determine intent, ideology, membership, or behavior. A count of keyword matches is not a risk score. Qualified human review and safeguarding remain necessary.
